SL review status

ICASSP 2027 Speech and Language track, from snapshots taken in CMS.

This page lists reviewer names and workloads, so it is encrypted. Nothing is readable until it is unlocked, and nothing you type leaves your browser.

What these numbers do not cover. Every paper figure here comes from the papers list in the CMS TC chair interface, and that list serves only papers carrying a 10.x EDICS. Paged in full on 20 November 2026 it returned 1,777 papers, while CMS reports 1,798 submissions for the track, so 21 papers are counted by CMS and never shown to the chair. Special session papers are the known case of papers that sit outside the chair's view, and nothing on this page says anything about their review status.

Each snapshot is one run of the status script against CMS. The reviewer assignment count in CMS is the total across all technical committees, so a reviewer may be carrying papers that are not ours.
